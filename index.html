    05

    Frameworks

    structure for speaking on the spot
    PREP
    Point → Reason → Example → Point
    Opinions, interview answers, Q&A
    STAR
    Situation → Task → Action → Result
    "Tell me about a time…" stories
    What / So What / Now What
    What happened → Why it matters → What's next
    Status updates, reflections
    Past / Present / Future
    Where it was → where it is → where it's going
    Project updates, narratives
    Problem / Solution / Benefit
    The pain → your fix → why it's worth it
    Pitches, persuasive asks
    Rule of Three
    Group any list into exactly three points
    Any speech — triads are what people retain

    Fallback for anything: signpost → body → recap. Even in 20 seconds — "Two things on this: [point one], [point two]. So bottom line: [recap]." It makes rambling structurally difficult.

    Phrasing for impact

    • Lead with the conclusion — say the point first, then justify it.
    • Cut hedges — "I think maybe we could possibly…" → just state it.
    • Concrete beats vague — name the number or result, not "things went well."
    • One idea per sentence when speaking — shorter than written sentences.
    • End on your strongest word, not a trailing qualifier.
    • Swap fillers for pauses — a silent half-second reads as confidence.

    Quick swaps

    "I just wanted to say…"(delete — just say it)
